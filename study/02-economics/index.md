# Economics — Module Overview

Currency exchange rate determination and the drivers of long-run economic growth.

---

## Module 1: Currency Exchange Rates

FX market mechanics and arbitrage, forward markets and mark-to-market valuation, the five international parity conditions and how they interlock, the FX carry trade, balance-of-payments and capital-flow drivers, monetary/fiscal policy transmission models, and central bank intervention with currency-crisis warning signs.

- **[FX Market Concepts and Arbitrage](/cfa/study/02-economics/01-currency-exchange-rates/01-fx-market-concepts-and-arbitrage/)** — Quote conventions, bid-offer spreads, real vs. nominal exchange rates, and triangular arbitrage
- **[Forward Markets and Parity Conditions](/cfa/study/02-economics/01-currency-exchange-rates/02-forward-markets-and-parity-conditions/)** — Forward points, covered interest rate parity, and mark-to-market forward valuation
- **[Uncovered Interest Rate Parity and Purchasing Power Parity](/cfa/study/02-economics/01-currency-exchange-rates/03-uncovered-parity-and-ppp/)** — Uncovered IRP, absolute/relative PPP, and the real exchange rate
- **[The Fisher Effect and Parity Conditions Synthesis](/cfa/study/02-economics/01-currency-exchange-rates/04-fisher-effect-and-parity-synthesis/)** — The international Fisher effect and how all parity conditions tie together
- **[The Carry Trade and Balance of Payments](/cfa/study/02-economics/01-currency-exchange-rates/05-carry-trade-and-balance-of-payments/)** — Carry trade mechanics, current account channels, and capital flows
- **[Exchange Rate Policy Models](/cfa/study/02-economics/01-currency-exchange-rates/06-exchange-rate-policy-models/)** — Mundell–Fleming, monetary models, and the portfolio balance approach
- **[Exchange Rate Management and Formula Summary](/cfa/study/02-economics/01-currency-exchange-rates/07-summary/)** — Intervention, capital controls, currency crisis warning signs, and all formulas in one place

## Module 2: Economic Growth

The drivers of long-run potential GDP growth — production-function/growth-accounting decomposition, natural resources, labor and capital inputs, and the classical, neoclassical (Solow), and endogenous growth models — culminating in the convergence hypotheses and the effects of trade openness on growth.

- **[Growth Fundamentals and Why Investors Care](/cfa/study/02-economics/02-economic-growth/01-growth-fundamentals-and-investors/)** — Developed vs. developing growth patterns, and factors favoring/limiting growth
- **[Production Function and Growth Accounting](/cfa/study/02-economics/02-economic-growth/02-production-function-and-growth-accounting/)** — The Cobb-Douglas production function and the growth accounting equation
- **[Growth Inputs: Natural Resources, Labor, and Capital](/cfa/study/02-economics/02-economic-growth/03-growth-inputs-labor-and-resources/)** — Natural resources, labor supply, human capital, and technology/infrastructure
- **[Classical and Neoclassical Growth Models](/cfa/study/02-economics/02-economic-growth/04-classical-and-neoclassical-growth-models/)** — The Malthusian model, the Solow model, and its steady-state implications
- **[Endogenous Growth and Convergence](/cfa/study/02-economics/02-economic-growth/05-endogenous-growth-and-convergence/)** — The endogenous growth model, convergence hypotheses, and growth in an open economy
- **[Formula Summary](/cfa/study/02-economics/02-economic-growth/06-summary/)** — All economic growth formulas and exam tips in one place
