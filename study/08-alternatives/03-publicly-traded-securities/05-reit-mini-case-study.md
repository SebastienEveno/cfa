## Summary: REIT Mini Case Study — Capitol Shopping Center REIT Inc. (CFA Level II — Alternative Investments)

---

### Company Overview

**CSC**: Fictitious retail REIT owning shopping centers primarily in Washington, DC metro area.

**Qualitative strengths:**
- **Defensive tenant base**: Grocery/drug store necessity retailers
- **Favorable location**: DC government employment stability + strict zoning limits new supply
- **Track record**: 2–3% annual rent/NOI growth over past decade
- **Outperformed peers** during difficult Years 1–2 (many peers saw FFO/AFFO decline; CSC grew)

### Recent Acquisition Example

**Transaction**: 3 shopping centers acquired mid-Year 2 for **$111.2 million**

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Going-in cap rate | **6.75%** |
| Market average cap rate | 6.0%–6.25% |
| Financing | $54.6M mortgage @ 6% + equity offering (1M shares) + cash |

**Why CSC achieved better-than-market cap rate**: Strong tenant/broker relationships, reputation, and **ability to move quickly** due to strong balance sheet — plus upside potential since property isn't fully leased.

### Selecting a Valuation Method — Key Guidance

**When multiple methods yield different results:**
1. **Re-examine assumptions** driving each approach
2. Consider which method has the **most reliable assumptions**
3. Consider which method **other investors** likely emphasize
4. Consider **analyst's own investment philosophy**

**Possible resolution approaches:**
- Use a **single preferred** method
- Take the **midpoint** of a range of values
- Use a **weighted average** based on perceived reliability

### Exam Tips

- **FFO = Net income + D&A** (simplified version without gain/loss adjustment shown here since CSC had no property sales)
- **AFFO = FFO − Non-cash rent − Recurring capex**
- **Non-cash rent** ≈ change in deferred rent receivable (the balance sheet line item)
- **Interest coverage = EBITDA/Interest expense** — CSC's 3.38× vs. peers' 2.35× shows meaningfully lower risk
- **Net debt/EBITDA** — lower is better; CSC's 5.01× vs. peers' 7.10× indicates conservative leverage
- **Premium/discount to peers on P/FFO or P/AFFO** signals relative market perception of quality, growth, and risk
- **CAPM required return** = $r_f + \beta \times MRP$ — use this for DCF discount rate if constructing a full valuation
- **Multiple valuation methods (NAV, P/FFO, DCF) can disagree** — analysts must reconcile assumptions rather than mechanically pick one number
- **Defensive REITs** (grocery-anchored, government-region located) tend to have **less downside but also less upside** compared to cyclical peers during recovery phases — reflected in CSC's more modest Yr4-5 growth vs. "All REITs"
